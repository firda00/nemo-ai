## Working positioning

One-line version:

> Nemo lets small businesses turn a written workflow into a supervised team of AI agents connected to their knowledge, messaging channels, and business tools.

Initial wedge for the application:

> Repetitive sales and operations workflows for education and service SMBs that already handle customer work in chat and spreadsheets.

Long-term vision:

> A universal, permission-bound platform for creating and operating AI workers for any measurable business workflow.

Do not lead the application with “AGI” or “agents for absolutely everything.” YC recommends concrete, matter-of-fact descriptions. The broader vision belongs after the current product, user, and wedge are clear.

## Evidence currently verified in the repository

- Working React/TypeScript product with a Supabase backend architecture.
- Agent and AI-team creation flows, knowledge, tools, channels, routing, human handoff, billing, observability, and eval code paths exist.
- Telegram, widget, WhatsApp, and Instagram webhook implementations exist in source.
- Production build passes.
- Browser smoke coverage passes 12 public, authenticated-demo, and mobile routes.
- Deterministic/live agent evaluation last passed 76/76 scenarios.
- Real agent execution trace is currently under implementation and independent Codex review.

These facts prove engineering progress, not traction. They must not be presented as customers, revenue, production usage, or product-market fit.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Never invent users, revenue, pilots, growth, partnerships, founder achievements, or incorporation details.
- Use exact numbers and dates where they exist.
- Separate “implemented,” “tested locally,” “used by a design partner,” and “running in production.”
- Keep the first sentence narrow and reproducible; put the universal-platform vision later.
- Submitting the application is an external action and requires final founder approval.
