# YC founder video and product demo plan

## One-minute founder video

YC asks for a one-minute video containing only the founders talking. All founders should appear. Do not use a product montage, music, slides, or a memorized script; speak naturally from bullet points.

Suggested bullet structure:

1. Names and the concrete thing each founder builds or owns.
2. “We are building Nemo, which turns a written business workflow into a supervised team of AI agents connected to company knowledge, messaging, and tools.”
3. The specific problem personally observed and initial customer segment.
4. One concrete proof point: real usage, measured outcome, or unusually fast technical progress.
5. Why this founder team is the right team.

Missing inputs: founder names, founder story, customer proof, and commitment status.

## Separate two-minute product demo

Only demonstrate features that work reliably on the recorded build.

### 0:00–0:15 — Problem and input

Show one real, narrow workflow description, preferably from a design partner. Example:

> Qualify incoming education leads, answer course questions from our documents, prepare the CRM record, and hand hot leads to a manager.

### 0:15–0:40 — Create the team

Show Nemo generating a team lead and specialists from the workflow. Briefly show editable goals, tools, knowledge, and approval boundaries.

### 0:40–1:05 — Connect context

Attach a small non-sensitive knowledge file and select a channel/tool. Avoid displaying credentials, production customer data, or internal prompts.

### 1:05–1:35 — Run a workflow

Submit a realistic input. Show routing to a specialist, the output, structured fields, and human handoff for a sensitive or uncertain step.

### 1:35–1:50 — Evidence and control

Show Agent Trace, the test/eval result, and pause or rollback controls. If any of these are incomplete by recording day, omit them rather than simulate them.

### 1:50–2:00 — Result

State one measured customer result. **[REAL METRIC REQUIRED]**

## Recording readiness checklist

- Public HTTPS deployment is stable.
- Demo account uses synthetic, non-sensitive data.
- The exact workflow succeeds three consecutive times.
- No browser console errors.
- No secrets or customer identifiers appear.
- Loading and failure states are present.
- Demo remains understandable without narration.
- Backup recording and backup deployment are available.
